# 0005: Sector-native coverage

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

Treat a followed sector as a first-class coverage surface with a shared edition brief and four visible Story Cluster scopes. Require documented breadth for sector-wide promotion and documented propagation for company-led or company-specific inclusion. Apply issuer concentration safeguards only when at least three issuers genuinely qualify.

## Rationale

Mirroring a sector's largest issuer would omit policy, supply-chain, pricing, capacity, and subindustry developments. A scope-native feed lets broad evidence remain prominent while preserving the company-led origin of issuer events and the specific transmission path of macro developments.

## Consequences

Sector Briefs are reusable by sector, date, and edition. Ranking retains pre- and post-diversity transparency. Coverage gaps remain visible. Strong material evidence is not sacrificed for artificial diversity.
