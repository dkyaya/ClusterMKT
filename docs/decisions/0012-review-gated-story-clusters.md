# 0012 — Gate Story Clusters through explicit review routing

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Not every candidate grouping has sufficient evidence, provenance, or event clarity to become visible or enter a Sector Brief.

## Decision

Assign every candidate a confidence level, review status, blocking issues, warnings, display-eligibility decision, and Sector Brief eligibility decision. Missing provenance, unsupported detail, metadata-only factual summaries, podcast-metadata claims, quarantined evidence, and unresolved boundaries block or require review.

## Consequences

Review-required, rejected, and quarantined candidates remain inspectable and countable without masquerading as accepted coverage. Sector Brief assembly consumes accepted eligible clusters only.
