# Source access boundaries

Cluster MKT separates knowing that a source exists from having permission and technical readiness to retrieve it. Registry presence, public accessibility, or a discoverable feed does not establish authorization.

The current product may use only reviewed offline fixture records. Metadata-only fixtures remain limited to discovery and source identity; inaccessible content is not summarized. Podcast metadata without an accessible permitted transcript remains related listening. Prohibited, disabled, unreviewed, quarantined, and dead-letter inputs cannot enter accepted Story Clusters or Sector Briefs.

Before any future live source is enabled, its terms, legal posture, technical capability, retrieval method, rate and user-agent requirements, attribution, update behavior, and provenance handling must be reviewed explicitly. No current source passes that live gate.
